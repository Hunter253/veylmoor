# Hidden places (25.a and 14.b Hidden places)

**What this batch is for:** Pieces for secret passages, inscriptions and hidden rewards players can find by exploring.

**Zone:** Veylmoor first; under Ideas, suggest colour changes for the other four zones.

**Existing models to reuse or match:** Match moor-wall.js, the dungeon-arch files and warden-beacon.js.

**Assets (name, budget, what it is, how it moves):**

1. `secret-wall-door.js`, budget **medium**: a section of old stone wall, about 2 wide and 2 tall, with one slab slightly out of line (the hidden door).
2. `inscribed-stone.js`, budget **medium**: a flat standing stone with carved rune marks (shapes only, no letters) glowing faint cyan (#47f8ff).
3. `hidden-cache.js`, budget **small**: a small iron-bound box tucked under a stone lip.
4. `loose-floor-slab.js`, budget **small**: a square floor slab lifted at one edge, a dark gap under it.
5. `crawl-gap.js`, budget **medium**: a low gap between fallen rocks, wide enough to crawl through, about 1.2 tall.

**Must include:** nothing that looks like a modern door or lock.

**Must avoid:** text or letters, real-world symbols, modern items, anything from another game or film, and the story characters (the Keeper, the masked envoys).
