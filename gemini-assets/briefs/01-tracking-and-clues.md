# Tracks, clues and search spots (12.b New quest types)

**What this batch is for:** Small ground props for new tracking, investigation and stealth quests.

**Zone:** Veylmoor first; the cover plant gets a version for each zone in brief 02.

**Existing models to reuse or match:** Match the existing small props such as treasure-chest.js and the res-*.js gathering nodes. Keep them flat and low so they read from above.

**Assets (name, budget, what it is, how it moves):**

1. `track-marks.js`, budget **small**: a short trail of three paw prints and two boot prints pressed into the ground, almost flat (under 0.05 tall). Doesn't move.
2. `clue-marker.js`, budget **small**: a small pile of disturbed things a player would stop and look at: a torn cloth scrap on a stick, a few scattered stones. Doesn't move.
3. `search-spot.js`, budget **small**: a patch of freshly dug earth with a half-buried wooden box corner showing. Doesn't move.
4. `hiding-reeds.js`, budget **medium**: a dense clump of tall moor reeds and grass, about 1.4 tall and 1.6 wide, big enough to crouch behind. Doesn't move (the game sways it).

**Must include:** a clear top view; colours that stand out a little from grass and mud so players can spot them.

**Must avoid:** text or letters, real-world symbols, modern items, anything from another game or film, and the story characters (the Keeper, the masked envoys).
