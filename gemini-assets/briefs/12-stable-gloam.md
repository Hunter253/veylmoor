# Stable kit: Gloamwood (28.a Stables)

**What this batch is for:** A stable in each town where mounts are kept and services are offered.

**Zone:** Gloamwood

**Existing models to reuse or match:** Match the zone's existing buildings in index.html and the Ridgehorn's size (1.8 long, 1.75 tall).

**Assets (name, budget, what it is, how it moves):**

1. `stable-gloam.js`, budget **house**: an open-fronted stable with two stalls, twisted dark wood, violet cloth, pale fungus on the beams. About 4 wide, 3 deep, 3 tall; the open side faces +z.
2. `hitching-post-gloam.js`, budget **small**: a hitching post with a rail, in the same style.
3. `water-trough-gloam.js`, budget **small**: a water trough in the same style.
4. `feed-stack-gloam.js`, budget **small**: a stack of feed (hay, grain sacks or the zone's equivalent).

**Must include:** room inside each stall for a Ridgehorn; nothing solid outside the stated footprint.

**Must avoid:** text or letters, real-world symbols, modern items, anything from another game or film, and the story characters (the Keeper, the masked envoys).
