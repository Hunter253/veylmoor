# Headwear for each class (4.a Appearance choices)

**What this batch is for:** One optional headwear piece per class.

**Zone:** None (worn by heroes).

**Existing models to reuse or match:** Same head frame as brief 04. Match each class's colours in index.html: MODELS.bulwark, reaver, ashcaller, thornstalker and lumen.

**Assets (name, budget, what it is, how it moves):**

1. `hat-bulwark.js`, budget **medium**: an open-faced iron cap with a brass rim.
2. `hat-reaver.js`, budget **medium**: a fur-trimmed leather hood, thrown back slightly.
3. `hat-ashcaller.js`, budget **medium**: a deep red cowl with a soot-dark edge and one tiny ember glow.
4. `hat-thornstalker.js`, budget **medium**: a leaf-green ranger's hood with a short peak.
5. `hat-lumen.js`, budget **medium**: a simple brass circlet with one small glowing stone.

**Must include:** aim 200 triangles each; it must sit over hair-short without the hair poking through.

**Must avoid:** text or letters, real-world symbols, modern items, anything from another game or film, and the story characters (the Keeper, the masked envoys).
