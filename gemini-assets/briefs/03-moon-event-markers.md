# Moon event markers (44 Rare moon events)

**What this batch is for:** A small marker that appears in each zone during four rare kinds of moonlit night, so players notice something special is happening.

**Zone:** Any; they appear in every zone, so keep them neutral in style.

**Existing models to reuse or match:** Match warden-beacon.js and waystone-obelisk.js for the Warden stone style.

**Assets (name, budget, what it is, how it moves):**

1. `moon-marker-red.js`, budget **medium**: a cracked standing stone, about 1.2 tall, with small deep-red glowing veins, for a red-moon night.
2. `moon-marker-eclipse.js`, budget **medium**: a low ring of dark stones around a black stone bowl, with a thin silver glow on the rim, for an eclipse night.
3. `moon-marker-stars.js`, budget **medium**: a slim crystal spire on a stone base, about 1.4 tall, with small pale-violet and white glowing points, for a star-filled night.
4. `moon-marker-ancient.js`, budget **medium**: an old Warden stone half-sunk in the ground, with cyan rune marks (#47f8ff) glowing, for a night when the old stones wake.

**Must include:** glow kept small. Under Ideas, suggest a sky colour for each of the four nights.

**Must avoid:** text or letters, real-world symbols, modern items, anything from another game or film, and the story characters (the Keeper, the masked envoys).
