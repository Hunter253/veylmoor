# Pets (27 Pets)

**What this batch is for:** Small companions that follow the hero.

**Zone:** As named below.

**Existing models to reuse or match:** familiar-cat.js and catModel() in index.html. Use these parts so the follow code can animate them: body, head, tail, lf, rf, lb, rb (a creature without legs uses body, head, tail only). About 0.5 to 0.7 tall.

**Assets (name, budget, what it is, how it moves):**

1. `pet-white-pup.js`, budget **pet**: Frostfang Reach: a small white wolf pup with oversized paws and ears.
2. `pet-moor-hare.js`, budget **pet**: Veylmoor: a brown moor hare with long ears.
3. `pet-sand-fox.js`, budget **pet**: Sunscar Dunes: a pale fennec-like fox with huge ears.
4. `pet-ember-salamander.js`, budget **pet**: Emberfall Caldera: a stubby black salamander with orange spots (tiny glow).

**Must include:** aim about 450 triangles each; describe idle, walk and a happy animation.

**Must avoid:** text or letters, real-world symbols, modern items, anything from another game or film, and the story characters (the Keeper, the masked envoys).
