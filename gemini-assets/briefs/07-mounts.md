# More mounts (26 More mounts)

**What this batch is for:** New mounts the hero can unlock and ride.

**Zone:** Recolours first; then one new mount each for Sunscar Dunes, Frostfang Reach and Gloamwood.

**Existing models to reuse or match:** The Ridgehorn: steedModel() in index.html and warden-steed.js. New mounts must use the same parts so the riding code works: body (with saddle), head, mane, tail, lf, rf, lb, rb. Saddle top at about y=1.35, centred at z=-0.05; about 1.75 tall to the head, 1.8 long.

**Assets (name, budget, what it is, how it moves):**

1. Ridgehorn recolours, **no file**: in your answer, suggest three colour sets for the Ridgehorn (coat, dark, mane, saddle) as [r,g,b] lists. Claude adds them in code.
2. `mount-dune-strider.js`, budget **mount**: a long-legged, sand-coloured desert beast with a low hump and wide feet.
3. `mount-frost-elk.js`, budget **mount**: a shaggy elk with broad antlers and a thick winter coat.
4. `mount-gloam-stag.js`, budget **mount**: a dark violet stag with pale fungus growing on its antlers (glow kept tiny).

**Must include:** the same 8 parts and joint positions as the Ridgehorn; aim about 1,100 triangles. For each, describe how it should move at walk and gallop.

**Must avoid:** text or letters, real-world symbols, modern items, anything from another game or film, and the story characters (the Keeper, the masked envoys).
