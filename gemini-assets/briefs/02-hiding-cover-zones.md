# Hiding cover for each zone (12.b New quest types)

**What this batch is for:** Crouch-behind cover so stealth quests work in every zone.

**Zone:** One per zone, as named below.

**Existing models to reuse or match:** Use hiding-reeds.js from brief 01 as the size guide: about 1.4 tall, 1.6 wide.

**Assets (name, budget, what it is, how it moves):**

1. `hiding-cover-dunes.js`, budget **medium**: Sunscar Dunes: a cluster of dry scrub and a leaning slab of sandstone.
2. `hiding-cover-frost.js`, budget **medium**: Frostfang Reach: a snow-laden low pine thicket with a drift at its base.
3. `hiding-cover-gloam.js`, budget **medium**: Gloamwood: a thicket of twisted violet brambles with a few pale fungus caps.
4. `hiding-cover-ember.js`, budget **medium**: Emberfall Caldera: a jumble of black rock with charred stumps and a faint orange crack (glow kept tiny).

**Must include:** each one solid enough to hide a crouching person (about 1.0 tall at the lowest point of cover).

**Must avoid:** text or letters, real-world symbols, modern items, anything from another game or film, and the story characters (the Keeper, the masked envoys).
