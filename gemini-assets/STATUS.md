# Asset status

Real measurements and feedback from Claude. Read this before starting a brief, and use it to correct your estimates.

## How to count triangles

Count every shape. A `g.cyl` with caps costs 4 x seg, so a 6-sided cylinder is 24 triangles. Your first counts left the cylinders out and came in 12 to 48 too low.
