Privacy

Wardens of Veylmoor · last updated 4 November 2026

The short version: your heroes and settings stay in your own browser. The game has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking cookies, and nothing you do in it is sent to me.

Who runs the game

Wardens of Veylmoor is made and run by Hunter Bourgoin. If you have a question about this page or your data, email wardensofveylmoor@gmail.com.

What the game stores, and where

Everything below is saved in your browser's storage on your own device. None of it is sent to me or to anyone else.

  • Your heroes: up to five save slots with each hero's progress, items and quests.
  • Your settings: sound, controls, text size, tips you have already seen and the other choices under Options.
  • The last update you read, so the game knows when to show you a new one.
  • The last 20 errors the game ran into, so a bug report can include them if you choose to send one.
  • An offline copy of the game (its page, pictures and lettering), so it still opens without an internet connection.

Because these stay on your device, I can't see them, and I can't get them back if they are lost.

Other services involved

  • Netlify hosts the game's files. Like any website host, it receives your IP address and basic browser details when your device downloads the game, and may keep them in its server logs. See Netlify's privacy policy.
  • GitHub is only involved if you press Report a bug. The game opens GitHub's bug form with your game version, device and browser type, how you were playing, your hero's level, class and area, and recent errors already filled in. Nothing is sent until you read the form and submit it yourself, and you can change or remove anything first. Bug reports on GitHub are public, and GitHub's privacy statement applies there.
  • Email. If you write to wardensofveylmoor@gmail.com, I see your email address and what you wrote. I use them only to read and reply to your message.

The game's lettering is served from the game's own site, so no font requests go to another company.

What the game does not do

  • No accounts or logins (yet; see below).
  • No advertising, analytics or tracking cookies.
  • No selling or sharing of your information.

Deleting your data

  • To erase one hero, open Options, then Game, and press Delete.
  • To erase everything, clear this site's data in your browser settings (often called Site settings or Clear browsing data). If you installed the game to your home screen, uninstalling it does the same.
  • To remove a bug report you sent, email me with its link and I'll delete it.

Children

The game does not ask for or collect personal information from anyone, children included. If accounts are added, this page will explain how they work for younger players before they open.

When accounts arrive

Accounts and cloud saves are planned for later. Before they launch, this page will be updated to say exactly what an account stores, which service keeps it, and how to delete it. Playing without an account will always stay possible.

Changes to this page

If this page changes, the date at the top changes too, and anything important will be announced in the game's Updates.
